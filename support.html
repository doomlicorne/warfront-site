《戰域紀元》玩家支援

買了完整版，換手機或重新安裝後沒有解鎖？

到主選單 › 商店，按「回復購買」。請確認使用的是當初購買的 Apple ID，或同一個家人共享群組。

遊戲進度會不會不見？

進度存在手機上，已登入 iCloud 時也會同步到你的其他裝置。換手機前請確認兩台都登入同一個 iCloud。

需要網路嗎？

不需要，全部內容都可以離線遊玩。

字太小？

設定 › 劇情文字大小可以選「特大」。

語言？

設定 › 語言可以即時切換繁體中文與英文。

其他問題或建議

請寄信至doomlicorne@gmail.com，附上裝置型號與遊戲版本（設定頁最下方的「版本」）。遇到錯誤時，可以在設定頁按「匯出遊玩紀錄」一併附上。

Era of the Rift — Support

Bought the Full Version, but it's locked after a new phone or reinstall?

Go to Main Menu › Store and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're using the Apple ID that made the purchase, or the same Family Sharing group.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices. Before switching phones, check that both are signed in to the same iCloud account.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything can be played offline.

Text too small?

Settings › Story Text Size › X-Large.

Language?

Settings › Language switches between English and Traditional Chinese instantly.

Other questions or feedback

Email doomlicorne@gmail.com with your device model and game version (the "Version" line at the bottom of Settings). If something went wrong, you can attach the file from "Export Play Log" in Settings.
