《戰域紀元》隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

《戰域紀元》（以下稱「本遊戲」）由TaiYuan Chen（以下稱「我們」）開發與發行。我們重視你的隱私，這份政策說明本遊戲如何處理資料。

一、我們不收集你的個人資料

本遊戲是單機遊戲，不需要帳號、不需要網路。我們不收集、不儲存、也不傳送任何可以識別你的資料，包括姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼或使用行為。本遊戲沒有廣告，也沒有使用任何第三方分析或追蹤工具。

二、只存在你裝置上的資料

為了讓遊戲運作，下列資料會存在你的裝置上，我們無法取得：

  • 遊戲進度與設定：戰役進度、牌組、陣營等級、音量與文字大小等。
  • 遊玩紀錄：對局的模式、牌組、結果與時長，搭配一組隨機產生、只用來把同一台裝置的紀錄串在一起的代碼（不是 Apple ID 或裝置識別碼）。這份紀錄不會自動傳送；只有你在設定頁按「匯出遊玩紀錄」、並自行寄給我們時，我們才會看到。

三、iCloud 同步

如果你的裝置已登入 iCloud，遊戲進度會透過 Apple 的 iCloud 同步到你自己的其他裝置。這些資料存在你的 iCloud 帳號中，由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，我們無法讀取。

四、購買

「完整版」的購買與付款全程由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。我們不會取得你的付款資訊，只會在裝置上確認你是否已經購買。

五、兒童隱私

本遊戲不收集任何使用者的個人資料，包括兒童。

六、刪除資料

刪除本遊戲即會移除裝置上的所有資料。iCloud 上的同步資料可以在 iPhone「設定」› 你的名字 › iCloud 中管理與刪除。

七、政策變更

如果未來的版本改變資料處理方式（例如加入連線功能），我們會先更新這份政策與 App Store 上的隱私標籤，並更新上方的生效日期。

八、聯絡我們

對這份政策有任何問題，請寄信至doomlicorne@gmail.com。

Era of the Rift — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Era of the Rift ("the Game") is developed and published by TaiYuan Chen ("we"). This policy explains how the Game handles data.

1. We do not collect personal data.

The Game is a single-player game that needs no account and no internet connection. We do not collect, store, or transmit any information that can identify you — including your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage behavior. The Game has no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

2. Data stored only on your device.

To work, the Game keeps the following on your device, where we cannot access it:

  • Game progress and settings, such as campaign progress, decks, faction levels, volume, text size, and language.
  • A play log: the mode, deck, result, and length of each match, together with a randomly generated code used only to group records from the same device (not your Apple ID or a device identifier). This log is never sent automatically. We only see it if you tap "Export Play Log" in Settings and send it to us yourself.

3. iCloud sync.

If your device is signed in to iCloud, your game progress syncs to your other devices through Apple's iCloud. This data lives in your iCloud account, is handled by Apple under its privacy policy, and cannot be read by us.

4. Purchases.

Purchases of the Full Version are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment information; the Game only checks on your device whether the Full Version has been purchased.

5. Children's privacy.

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Deleting data.

Deleting the Game removes all of its data from your device. Synced data in iCloud can be managed and deleted in your iPhone's Settings › your name › iCloud.

7. Changes.

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, by adding online features), we will first update this policy and the App Store privacy label, and update the effective date above.

8. Contact.

Questions about this policy: doomlicorne@gmail.com.
